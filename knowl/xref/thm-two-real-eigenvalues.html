Theorem 5.5.2. The Eigenvalue Method for Two Distinct Real Eigenvalues.

Suppose \(A\) is a \(2 \times 2\) matrix with two distinct, real eigenvalues \(\lambda_1, \lambda_2\) with corresponding eigenvectors \(\mathbf{v}_1, \mathbf{v}_2\text{.}\) Then the general solution of
\begin{equation*} \frac{d\mathbf{x}}{dt} = A\mathbf{x} \end{equation*}
is
\begin{equation*} \mathbf{x}(t) = c_1\mathbf{v}_1 e^{\lambda_1 t} + c_2\mathbf{v}_2 e^{\lambda_2 t}. \end{equation*}
