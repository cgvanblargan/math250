Example 3.2.1. A 10 kg Mass on a Spring.

A block of mass \(m = 10\) kg is attached to a spring with spring constant \(k = 140\) N/m. Find the general solution \(x(t)\) describing its position.
🔗
Hint.
Plug \(m = 10\) and \(k = 140\) into \(mx'' + kx = 0\) and solve.
🔗
🔗
Solution.
Substituting \(m = 10\) and \(k = 140\text{:}\)
\begin{equation*} 10x'' + 140x = 0. \end{equation*}
Dividing through by 10:
\begin{equation*} x'' + 14x = 0. \end{equation*}
So \(\omega_0^2 = 14\text{,}\) giving \(\omega_0 = \sqrt{14}\text{.}\) The general solution is:
\begin{equation*} x(t) = c_1\cos\!\left(\sqrt{14}\, t\right) + c_2\sin\!\left(\sqrt{14}\, t\right). \end{equation*}
