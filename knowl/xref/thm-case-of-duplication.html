Theorem 3.5.2. Case of Duplication.

Suppose the homogeneous equation \(ay'' + by' + cy = 0\) has general solution containing \(c_1\cos(kt) + c_2\sin(kt)\text{.}\) Then the guess \(y_p = A\cos(kt) + B\sin(kt)\) will always give \(0\) on the left-hand side of
\begin{equation*} ay'' + by' + cy = m_1\cos(kt) + m_2\sin(kt). \end{equation*}
This occurs specifically when the ODE can be written in the form:
\begin{equation*} y'' + k^2y = m_1\cos(kt) + m_2\sin(kt). \end{equation*}
In this case, use the modified guess:
\begin{equation*} y_p = t\bigl(A\cos(kt) + B\sin(kt)\bigr). \end{equation*}
