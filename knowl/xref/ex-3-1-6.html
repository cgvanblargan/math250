Exercise 5. From a General Solution Back to the ODE.

Suppose the general solution to a second-order linear homogeneous ODE with constant coefficients \(ay'' + by' + cy = 0\) (with \(a=1\)) is
\begin{equation*} y(x) = c_1 e^{8x} + c_2 e^{-8x}. \end{equation*}
🔗

(a)

What are the roots \(r_1\) and \(r_2\) of the characteristic equation?
🔗
🔗

(b)

Write the characteristic equation in the factored form \((r - r_1)(r - r_2) = 0\text{,}\) then expand it into the form \(r^2 + br + c = 0\text{.}\)
🔗
Hint.
Recall that for \(ay'' + by' + cy = 0\text{,}\) the characteristic equation is \(ar^2 + br + c = 0\text{,}\) and it factors as \(a(r-r_1)(r-r_2) = 0\) when \(r_1, r_2\) are the roots. With \(a = 1\text{,}\) just expand the product.
🔗
🔗
🔗

(c)

Using part (b), write down the ODE \(y'' + by' + cy = 0\) that has \(y(x) = c_1 e^{8x} + c_2 e^{-8x}\) as its general solution.
