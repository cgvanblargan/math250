Observation 3.1.2. The ODE \(y'' + \omega^2 y = 0\).

For any constant \(\omega \neq 0\text{,}\) the ODE
\begin{equation*} y'' + \omega^2 y = 0 \end{equation*}
has characteristic equation \(r^2 + \omega^2 = 0\text{,}\) giving roots \(r = \pm \omega i\) (so \(\alpha = 0\text{,}\) \(\beta = \omega\)). The general solution is:
\begin{equation*} y = c_1\cos(\omega x) + c_2\sin(\omega x). \end{equation*}
