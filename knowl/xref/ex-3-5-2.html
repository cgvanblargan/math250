Exercise 2. Normal Case vs. Case of Duplication.

Consider the ODE
\begin{equation*} my'' + 8y = 8\cos(2t), \end{equation*}
where \(m\) is a positive constant.
🔗

(a)

For what value of \(m\) are we in the case of duplication?
🔗
Hint.
Divide through by \(m\) first to put the ODE in standard form, then check whether the coefficient of \(y\) equals \(k^2\text{,}\) where \(k = 2\) is the frequency on the right-hand side.
