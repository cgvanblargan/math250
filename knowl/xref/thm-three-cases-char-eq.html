Theorem 3.1.1. Solving \(ay'' + by' + cy = 0\text{:}\) All Three Cases.

Consider the characteristic equation \(ar^2 + br + c = 0\text{.}\) In each case below, we find two linearly independent solutions \(y_1\) and \(y_2\text{.}\)
🔗
  • Two distinct real roots \(r_1 \neq r_2\text{:}\)
    \begin{equation*} y_1 = e^{r_1 x}, \qquad y_2 = e^{r_2 x} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  • One repeated real root \(r_1\text{:}\)
    \begin{equation*} y_1 = e^{r_1 x}, \qquad y_2 = xe^{r_1 x} \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  • Two complex conjugate roots \(\alpha \pm \beta i\text{:}\)
    \begin{equation*} y_1 = e^{\alpha x}\cos(\beta x), \qquad y_2 = e^{\alpha x}\sin(\beta x) \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
In each case, the general solution is \(y = c_1 y_1 + c_2 y_2\text{.}\)
