Theorem 3.6.1. General Solution: Resonance vs. Non-Resonance.

Consider the forced undamped system
\begin{equation*} mx'' + kx = F_0\cos(\omega t) + G_0\sin(\omega t), \quad \omega_0 = \sqrt{\frac{k}{m}}. \end{equation*}
🔗
  • Non-resonance (\(\omega \neq \omega_0\)):
    \begin{equation*} x(t) = \underbrace{c_1\cos(\omega_0 t) + c_2\sin(\omega_0 t)}_{x_c} + \underbrace{A\cos(\omega t) + B\sin(\omega t)}_{x_p}. \end{equation*}

    🔗
  • Resonance (\(\omega = \omega_0\)):
    \begin{equation*} x(t) = \underbrace{c_1\cos(\omega_0 t) + c_2\sin(\omega_0 t)}_{x_c} + \underbrace{t\bigl(A\cos(\omega_0 t) + B\sin(\omega_0 t)\bigr)}_{x_p}. \end{equation*}
