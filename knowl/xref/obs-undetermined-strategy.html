Observation 3.5.2. A Useful Strategy for Most Non-Homogeneous Equations.

To find a particular solution \(y_p\) to
\begin{equation*} ay'' + by' + cy = m_1\cos(kt) + m_2\sin(kt), \end{equation*}
guess
\begin{equation*} y_p = A\cos(kt) + B\sin(kt), \end{equation*}
substitute into the ODE, match coefficients of \(\sin(kt)\) and \(\cos(kt)\) on both sides, and solve for \(A\) and \(B\text{.}\) This works in most cases (see note above about duplication).
