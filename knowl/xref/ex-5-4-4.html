Exercise 4.

Find the eigenvalues of \(A = \begin{bmatrix} 0 \amp -4 \\ 4 \amp 0 \end{bmatrix}\text{.}\) (As in the last problem, we’ll find the eigenvectors in the next section.)
🔗
Hint.
Compute \(\det(A - \lambda I)\text{.}\) Notice the diagonal entries of \(A\) are both \(0\text{,}\) so the resulting quadratic has no \(\lambda\) (linear) term. You can solve it directly without the quadratic formula, but the quadratic formula always works if preferred.
