Fact 3.2.2. Hooke’s Law.

The restoring force exerted by a spring is proportional to the displacement \(x(t)\) from equilibrium:
\begin{equation*} F_s = -kx, \end{equation*}
where \(k \gt 0\) is the spring constant. The negative sign means the force always pulls the mass back toward equilibrium.
