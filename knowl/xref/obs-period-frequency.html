Observation 3.2.4. Period and Frequency of Simple Harmonic Motion.

For simple harmonic motion \(x(t) = c_1\cos(\omega_0 t) + c_2\sin(\omega_0 t)\text{:}\)
  • The period (time to complete one full oscillation) is:
    \begin{equation*} T = \frac{2\pi}{\omega_0}. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
  • The frequency (number of oscillations per unit time) is:
    \begin{equation*} f = \frac{\omega_0}{2\pi} = \frac{1}{T}. \end{equation*}
    🔗
    🔗
Larger \(\omega_0\) means faster oscillation: shorter period, higher frequency.
