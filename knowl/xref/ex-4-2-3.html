Exercise 3.

Find the inverse Laplace transform of the following function.
\begin{equation*} F(s) = \frac{2s}{(s^2+1)(s^2+9)} \end{equation*}
🔗
Hint.
This denominator has two irreducible quadratic factors, a case we did not see in the notes. Each irreducible quadratic contributes a fraction with a linear numerator, just as before:
\begin{equation*} \frac{2s}{(s^2+1)(s^2+9)} = \frac{As+B}{s^2+1} + \frac{Cs+D}{s^2+9}. \end{equation*}
Clear denominators and match coefficients of each power of \(s\) to solve for the four constants.
