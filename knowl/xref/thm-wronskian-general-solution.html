Theorem 5.3.7. The Wronskian and the General Solution.

If \(\mathbf{x}\) and \(\mathbf{y}\) are two solutions to a first-order, linear, homogeneous system such that:
\begin{equation*} W(\mathbf{x}, \mathbf{y}) \neq 0, \end{equation*}
then the general solution is given by \(c_1\mathbf{x} + c_2\mathbf{y}\text{.}\)
